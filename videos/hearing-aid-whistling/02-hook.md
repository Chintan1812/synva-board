FULL HOOK (READ-THROUGH):

*[SFX: whistle, three seconds, cut to silence]*

That is not your hearing aid breaking.

It happens when you hug someone. In a full room, everybody turns to look.

So you turn the volume down. Now it is useless. Thirty thousand rupees, useless.

Nine times out of ten, this costs nothing. No new device. No new part.

And if you are the tenth? One ten second test finds it. You cannot do that at home.

ON SCREEN

- **On-screen line:** "That whistle is not a fault." *(6 words)*
- **Hook board:** a **myth-vs-truth card**, landscape, self-contained. Two panels.
  **WHAT YOU THINK: the device is faulty.** / **WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS: sound is leaking out
  and going back in.** Nothing else on it. No cause, no fix, no percentage. The board must
  not answer the question the hook just asked.
  *(Not the headline-specs board of rule 7. This is a concept video, no models are compared.)*
- **Delivery note:** the whistle must be **real, captured on the day, not a stock tone**.
  Recognition is the entire pattern interrupt. A synthesised beep does not sound like the
  thing that embarrasses them.
- **Loop anchors:**

  | Loop | Closes on node |

---

QUALITY CHECK

1. **Stranger test:** pass. The SFX is the hook. Anyone who owns a whistling hearing aid
   recognises it in under a second and cannot scroll past it.
2. **"So what" test:** pass. Every sentence earns its place. "Now it is useless" is the
   line that converts a mild annoyance into a financial loss.
3. **Specificity test:** pass. "hug someone" not "close to people". "Thirty thousand
   rupees" not "a lot of money", and the figure is grounded, Chintan states this is most
   common in basic hearing aids below ₹30,000. "Ten second test" not "a quick check".
4. **BS-detector test:** pass, **conditionally.** "Nine times out of ten this costs
   nothing" is a large claim. It survives only because an audiologist says it on camera
   and then *proves* it by making a device whistle and fixing it by hand. If that demo is
   not in the cut, this line must come out.
5. **Promise-delivery test:** pass. The video delivers the free fix at the ~90% node and
   the 10 second test at the `critical-gain` node. Nothing is promised that is not shown.

⚠️ **One thing deliberately NOT in the hook:** the cause. Hard rule 1 says the hook is
about the viewer's pain, not the solution. Naming "you are not putting it in properly" in
the first 30 seconds ends the video at 30 seconds. It is also the one line that could read
as blame, so it needs the definition beat before it, not before the trust beat.
