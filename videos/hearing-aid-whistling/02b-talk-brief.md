TALK BRIEF — the whistling video
=================================
Pointers, not a script. Percentages are where each beat lands in the runtime.
Talk track comes from `01-research.md`. **Boards are English, you speak Hindi. Never read
the board aloud.**

## Standing rules for this shoot

- **The word is "citi bajne vaali aawaz".** Not "feedback" when talking to the viewer.
  Say "feedback" exactly once, in the definition, then drop it.
- **Never blame the viewer, and never imply Synva mis-fits.** The line is *"nobody showed
  you the two second check"*, never *"you did it wrong"*. And on the dome: *"your
  audiologist already got this right"*, never *"the dome was wrong"*.
- **No hard percentage.** Say **"nine times out of ten"**. Never "95%", never a decimal.
- **Akriti is BTE only.** One demo, correct size dome, plus the ear impression. She is
  not a test bench for every device.
- **The whistle must be real audio**, captured on the day.

## 0% · HOOK

Frozen in `02-hook.md`. Deliver it verbatim.
⚠️ **Do not name the cause.** Point at the sound, not the reason.

## 5% · TRUST, fast

Name, founder, audiologist, 1000+ people in two years. Twenty seconds. Move.

## 8% · DEFINITION, and get out

Sound leaves the speaker. It leaks out of your ear. It goes back into the microphone.
It loops. That loop is called feedback.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"Remember that word. Leak. Every single fix in this video attacks the
> leak. Not one of them attacks the machine."*

## 15% · WHERE IT HAPPENS

BTE most of all, and **most of all in basic hearing aids below thirty thousand**. RIC
yes, and for a reason I will come to. Custom in-the-ear generally should not, because it
is moulded to your ear. Instant-fit will not, if it is actually sitting inside the ear.

**Say this out loud, it defuses a false alarm:** *any* basic device whistles when you
pull it out of your ear. That is physics, not a fault.

Devices with no feedback cancellation do it more. High-end devices almost never, unless
the power is very high, which means profound loss.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"If you own a BTE under thirty thousand rupees, the next ninety
> seconds are the whole video for you."*

## 28% · THE CAUSE, nine times out of ten  ← the payoff

The bud is loose. It is not going in properly.
**Demonstrate it.** Make it whistle on camera. Re-seat it. Silence. Two seconds.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"That is nine out of ten cases, solved, right there. Do not spend a
> single rupee yet. Pause this and go try it."*

Then: the rest is an actually wrong size, and that is rare.

## 40% · BTE SOLUTION, with Akriti

Correct size dome on a BTE. Akriti demonstrates. One thing, done properly.

## 50% · WHEN A DOME IS NOT ENOUGH: the mould

Profound loss means high power, and a rubber dome cannot hold that much sound in.
Akriti takes **your** ear impression on camera. Then the finished custom mould goes in.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"This is what your ear needs when a rubber dome physically cannot hold
> that much power. And no, you cannot buy this online."*

## 65% · RIC DOMES: on the board, not in your hand  ← the reversal

Board on screen, all the dome types at once. Then the hard turn:

**Dome change is almost never the solution.** Your audiologist already chose that dome
for your hearing loss. **Do not change it yourself.**

Why: on a **sloping loss**, a **vented** dome is deliberate. The vent is what lets you
keep natural sound. Seal it up to stop a whistle and you get **occlusion**, your own
voice booming inside your head. You traded one problem for a worse one.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"So it is not the machine. It is not the dome. If re-fitting it did not
> work, then something else is happening, and there is exactly one way to find it."*

## 80% · CRITICAL GAIN MEASUREMENT  ← closes the hook's loop

Your audiologist plays a sound through the hearing aid and measures how much comes back
into the microphone. The software then sets the ceiling on how loud the device is allowed
to get.

- **Phonak calls it "Feedback and real ear test". Signia calls it "Critical Gain
  Measurement".** Same job, two names. *(Saying both is a credibility beat. Nobody in the
  Indian set does this.)*
- **It takes ten seconds.** The only thing it costs you is the travel.
- **It matters most on RIC**, because that is where vented domes cannot be fully sealed.

> 🎣 **RE-HOOK:** *"Ten seconds. That is the whole test. The travel to your audiologist is
> longer than the test."*

## 92% · EMBEDDED TRUTH + CTA

Your own line, already written, use it as is:

> *"Isliye device ke saath-saath, audiologist ka experience aur after-care matter karta
> hai. Fitting, follow-up, adjustments, yeh sab milke hearing aid ko successful banate hai."*

CTA is **"bring it in and let us measure it"**, not "buy". This viewer already owns a
device.

## Do not say

- Any exact percentage. "Nine times out of ten" only.
- "You fitted it wrong." Say nobody showed you the check.
- "The dome was the wrong size." Wrong size is the rare case, not the story.
- "Feedback" to the viewer after the definition beat. It is "citi".
- Anything implying a whistle means a bad or cheap device. It does not.
