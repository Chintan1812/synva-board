CHOSEN: **The Hearing Aid You Bought Online Isn't Working. Here's Why.**

WHY: It carries the same content as the original but enters through a symptom the
viewer already has instead of an abstract safety question nobody searches. It scores
highest, it keeps the 1SD pain — the shame of having already made a ₹-thousands mistake
— instead of addressing a purchase that hasn't happened yet, and, decisively, **it sits
in Synva's own proven troubleshooting cluster**: "Bought NEW hearing aids? And Speech is
still not clear?" already did 3,376 views at 8.04 breakout with the same shape. This is
a repeat of something that worked here, not a bet on someone else's pattern.

AGAINST THE DATA: keep making it despite zero keyword volume. This topic is
browse-and-suggested, not search, and the zero is partly *why* it's worth doing — no
competitor in the set has covered it. The demand is real in the clinic even though it
is invisible in the keyword tool.

POSITION (CONFIRMED BY CHINTAN 2026-07-28): the risk is **the cheap ones on Amazon, and
buying anything without a fitting done by an audiologist.** Not the internet itself —
Synva sells through online consultation, so "online is unsafe" would argue against the
business. The line is: an unfitted device is the problem, wherever you bought it.
