Pointers, not a script. Walk the FigJam map left to right; the % is where each board

---

## The retention problem, and how this map solves it

A four-model comparison leaks viewers the moment someone decides which model is theirs.
Three mechanisms are built in. If you drop one, the video sags in the middle.

1. **The box stays in your hands the entire video.** Do not put it down after the
   unboxing. It is the only thing on screen a spec table cannot give you, and it is what
   makes this different from the two Infinio videos that already died on the channel.
2. **Three loops, all planted in the hook, none paid before 55%.** Nobody can self-select
   out while all three are open. Loop A pays at 55%, loop C at 70%, loop B at 78%.
3. **The surprise is placed at 70%,** past where comparison videos normally die, and it
   is an *inversion* rather than a ranking: the cheaper device is the better buy.

**The one thing that would break it:** resolving the I70 → I90 step early. Every rung
before it is setup. If you answer it on the ladder board at 34%, the next four minutes
have nothing to hold anyone.

---

## Standing rules for the whole shoot

- **Additive, never comparative-negative.** "This one adds X", never "that one is worse".
  Someone watching already owns the I30.
- **Boards are English, you are Hindi.** Never read a board aloud.
- **Say "MRP per pair" once, at 0%.** Never repeat it on every price board.
- **Attribute Phonak's claims.** "3x more likely", "35% less effort" are *their* numbers.
  Say "Phonak says". Never state them as measured fact.
- **Never say the I90 is a rip-off.** Show what it adds, show what it costs, stop talking.
  The arithmetic is the viewer's to do, and that restraint is the whole brand.
- ⚠️ **Decide MRP vs after-discount before you roll.** Every board prints MRP. If the
  shoot uses after-discount pricing, all eleven boards regenerate and the hook is re-read.

## 11% · inbox — open the box

- **This is the literal payoff of the title. Do it early and completely.**
- Receivers are ordered separately and chosen by hearing loss, not by price level. Most
  people assume the expensive level ships a better receiver. It does not.
- ChargerGo is the charger with a battery in it, ₹6,000 more, same hearing aid.
- **Land the hinge:** nothing in this box changes between I30 and I90. Same body, same
  charger, same contents. Only the software differs. That is what makes the price ladder
  a question worth asking, and it is why this board runs before the ladder.

## 22% · common — the shared floor

- Say the two upsell-killers plainly, because they are the two reasons people get talked
  upward inside this family and neither is true:
  - **Bluetooth is identical on all four.** 5.3, line-wide.
  - **Fitting range is identical on all four.** 0 to 110 dB. Your hearing loss does not
    decide your level.
- Name **AutoSense OS 7.0** once here. It comes back at 86%.
- ⚠️ The catalogue still says 6.0 and is wrong. The board prints 7.0. Do not read 6.0.

## 34% · ladder — the hub

- **Reveal one rung at a time with the veils.** Do not show the whole board at once.
- I30 → I50: three features and four channels for ₹1,02,000.
- **I50 → I70 is the real jump.** Four features, four channels, a year more warranty.
  If someone only remembers one step, this is the one worth the money.
- ⚠️ **Stop at the I70 → I90 rung. Name that it is coming, do not open it.**
- The car analogy lands here, and it is already proven on your camera
  (ClickUp `197p90-8276`): Creta / C-Class / S-Class / Maybach.

## 46% · sphere — where the AI actually is

- ⚠️ **The correction that matters: the I70 and I90 do not have AI.** Only the -Sphere
  versions do. This is the belief most buyers hold and it is wrong.
- DEEPSONIC is a **second chip**, not a setting and not a firmware mode.
- Phonak grades it honestly themselves: medium strength on I70-Sphere, full on I90-Sphere.
- Attribute the claims.

## 55% · turn — the payoff of loop A

- **Lift the veil that has been on since 0%.** This is the moment the hook promised.
- **Two things, not one.** Speech Enhancer, plus the eighth AutoSense program. The
  catalogue only records the first; Phonak's document records both. Say two.
- **Name the source out loud:** "this is Phonak's own chart, not my opinion."
- Same 20 channels. Same 3 year warranty. Let the silence do the work.

## 62% · battery — the honest limitation

- 56 hours normally. **10 to 11 hours with Sphere running continuously.**
- The thing you pay the premium for is the thing that drains the battery.
- Framing is "know this before you buy", never "don't buy it".
- If you are out in noise 14 hours a day, ask about this specifically in the fitting.

## 70% · dominated — the inversion, loop C

- **Slow down. This is the most useful forty seconds in the video.**
- I90-R ₹6,70,000. I70-Sphere ₹5,90,000. The Sphere is **₹80,000 cheaper**.
- What the I90-R adds over the I70-R is loud-noise handling. That is exactly what the
  Sphere's chip is built for, and it does it with dedicated silicon.
- The Sphere also carries **4 years of warranty against the I90-R's 3**.
- ⚠️ **BLOCKED:** if there is a real clinical reason to fit an I90-R over an I70-Sphere,
  it goes here as a fourth row and the board's title softens. Answer needed.

## 78% · verdict — loop B

- Say the permission line: going above what your day requires is not mandatory.
- ⚠️ **BLOCKED — placeholder board.** Five `[ASK CHINTAN]` rows need your answers before
