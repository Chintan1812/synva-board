# Talk brief — Phonak ₹50,000 vs ₹1,00,000

Pointers, not a script. Walk the FigJam map left to right; the % is where each board
lands. Talk tracks come from `mindmap.json`, corrected where the boards changed.

---

## Standing rules for the whole shoot

- **Additive, never comparative-negative.** "This one adds X", never "that one is worse".
  Someone watching already owns the cheap one.
- **Say the MRP thing ONCE**, at 22%. Never repeat it on a price board.
- **Never say "Phonak has no rechargeable BTE".** It has one, Naida L30-PR. The true claim
  is narrower: profound *power* and rechargeable never meet.
- **Signia gets one sentence, twice** (33% and 85%). Say it, move on. This is a Phonak
  video, not a comparison video.
- **Boards are English, you are Hindi.** Never read the board aloud.

## 18% · framework — the 3 steps

## 22% · mrp-note — the pricing disclaimer

- Every price you see is MRP. What you pay is less, and every clinic differs.
- Ask your clinic. **Say it once.**

## 26% · band50 — the ₹50,000 three

- Same money, three devices. Veil them one at a time.
- Terra: 8 channels, no Bluetooth, no rechargeable.
- Orion 50: rechargeable, and the one that actually sells here.
- Sirion 75: 12 channels and Bluetooth, but batteries.
- **Terra and Orion perform similarly; Sirion a little better.** Say this plainly before
  any spec talk — it stops the numbers leading.

## 33% · turn50 — THE TURN ⚡

- Rechargeable chahiye? Phonak can't, at this price. Bluetooth? Also no.
- **So at ₹50,000 we honestly recommend Signia.**
- One sentence. Do not linger, do not keep comparing.

## 45% · band1l — the ₹1,00,000 segment

- At this money the choice becomes **the body**, not performance.
- Audeo I30-R Go sells most — same aid as the I30-R, pocket charger.
- L30 is the Lumity fallback on a tighter budget. Virto for custom, Naida for power.

## 58% · delta — what the extra buys

- Four rungs: **Terra → Terra+ → Lumity → Infinio.**
- Terra → Terra+ is **marginal**: you buy Bluetooth, not better sound. And it has already
  left the ₹50,000 band.
- Terra+ → ₹1,00,000 is **meaningful**, and here is the line no competitor can say:
  *on Terra the AutoSense is "Limited" — in the fitting software I can barely change the
  environmental programs. The money buys headroom for me to tune it for you.*
- Roger is the meetings feature. Does not exist below this tier.
