## THE OPEN LOOP METHOD (how these were chosen)

A loop is only worth planting if it satisfies three tests. Each of the three below passes
all three; a fourth candidate was cut because it failed the second.

1. **It must be a question the viewer cannot answer themselves.** Not "what is a
   channel" — they can google that. "Which one does he tell people NOT to buy" is
   unguessable, because it comes from inside the practice.
2. **It must close on a board that already exists in the map.** A loop with no node
   never closes, and an unclosed loop is worse than no loop — it teaches the viewer that
   your promises do not pay off.
3. **Closing it must cost Synva something.** A loop that pays off in Synva's favour is an
   ad. A loop that pays off against Synva's short-term interest is the reason people
   subscribe. Both loops below cost money to close.

**Placement follows the retention clock** (see the video-mindmap skill): loops open in the
first 30 seconds, get one visible re-tease at the ~33% and ~66% branch transitions, and
close at ~85%, before the CTA. Loops are worth up to +32% watch time, but only if the
viewer is reminded they are still open.

**Cut candidate:** *"and the one brand that does what Phonak can't"* — fails test 3 in
reverse. It is a strong loop, but closing it makes the video a Signia advert, which
§4-FRAME rules out. The limitation gets stated as a Phonak fact instead, with no
suspense built around a competitor.

## QUALITY CHECK

1. **Stranger test — PASS.** "Both Phonaks fit the same hearing loss" is a factual claim
   that contradicts what a buyer assumes, with no context needed. It creates an immediate
   "wait, what am I paying for then?"
2. **"So what" test — PASS.** Every sentence advances: claim → why it matters to you →
   the specific thing you cannot buy → what you will be able to do.
3. **Specificity test — PASS.** Real prices, not "cheap" and "expensive". "Working with
   any phone" instead of "connectivity". "Two quotes" instead of "options".
4. **BS-detector test — PASS, and this is the hook's strength.** Every claim is checkable
   on Phonak's own site: identical 0-110 dB fitting range across both bands, and Terra
   listing no Bluetooth. A skeptical viewer who goes to verify comes back convinced.
5. **Promise-delivery test — PASS.** The video delivers exactly the two promised things:
   what each price buys (§1, §4b) and the two Phonak limitations (§4a). No overpromise.
