**So the cross-brand board must compare on:**
- **manufacturer specs that genuinely align** — channels, Bluetooth type, battery,
  rechargeable, warranty, fitting range
- **capability in plain language** — "adjusts automatically", "reduces background noise",
  "streams from the phone" — with the vendor name in small text underneath if at all

(This is the *brand-name decoder* problem from `videos/TOPIC-SLATE.md` showing up early.
Solving it here produces the asset for that video too.)

**One genuinely useful asymmetry:** Sirion lists **TV Sound compatibility** and Terra+
does not. For **L1 Calm & Comfortable — the TV-in-the-living-room lifestyle — that is the
single most relevant feature in the whole comparison.**
